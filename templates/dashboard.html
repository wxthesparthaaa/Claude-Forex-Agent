Settings
Autopilot
Kill switch{{ ' -- ENGAGED' if kill_switch_engaged else '' }}

Emergency stop -- when on, Autopilot will not place any new trades, regardless of confidence or phase. Existing open trades are unaffected and stay broker-protected by their own SL/TP. Switch off to resume.

Carry trade: AUD_JPY / CAD_JPY

Off by default (autopilot only). A genuinely different strategy from everything else this bot trades: instead of predicting price direction, it goes long whichever of AUD_JPY / CAD_JPY currently pays positive daily rollover (OANDA's live financing rate), and holds for weeks -- not hours -- collecting that rate differential. Backtested against a real historical rate reconstruction (not just today's rate): both pairs positive in 6-7 of the last 9 calendar years on price alone. Exits are NOT a fixed take-profit -- a scheduled check closes the position when realized volatility spikes into a risk-off regime (a hysteresis band prevents flip-flopping open/closed right at that threshold), or if the live rate direction itself reverses. A wide stop-loss is still attached to every order as a rare disaster backstop only. Both pairs share a "short JPY" leg -- a sudden broad yen strengthening (the real August 2024 carry unwind is the precedent) would hit both at once, which is exactly what this account's existing per-currency exposure cap already limits.

Cancel all trades before weekend close

On by default. Closes every open trade 10 minutes before forex closes for the weekend (Friday ~5pm New York), so nothing carries gap risk into Monday's reopen.

A Telegram summary confirming Autopilot is still scanning even when nothing qualifies to trade -- separate from trade alerts, which always send immediately regardless of this setting.
